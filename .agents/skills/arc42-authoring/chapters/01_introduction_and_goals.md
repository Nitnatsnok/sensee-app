# Chapter 1 — Introduction and Goals (Введение и цели)

## Purpose
State the driving requirements and forces architects must consider: main business goals, essential features, core functional requirements, the top quality goals, and the stakeholders with their expectations.

## Required / recommended subsections
- **Requirements Overview** — short summary of functional requirements and drivers; link to any requirements documents rather than restating them.
- **Quality Goals** — the top 3–5 *architecture* quality goals (not project goals), each ideally with a concrete scenario; base the categories on ISO 25010. These are the goals chapter 10 elaborates.
- **Stakeholders** — table: role/name · contact · expectations toward the architecture and its documentation.

## Form
Short prose plus tables (use-case-style requirements overview; quality-goal table ordered by priority; stakeholder table).

## Do not put here
- Detailed quality scenarios and the full quality tree (→ §10, which references these goals).
- Constraints (→ §2). The solution approach (→ §4).

## Related chapters
§10 (Quality Requirements elaborates these goals), §2 (Constraints), §4 (Solution Strategy).
