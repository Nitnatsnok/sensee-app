# Chapter 11 — Technical Risks and Technical Debt (Риски и технический долг)

## Purpose
List identified technical risks and technical debt, ordered by priority, with suggested mitigation/avoidance measures — input for risk management.

## Required / recommended subsections
- A prioritized risk/debt list or table; optional mitigation per item.
- **Intentional early-stage relaxations** belong here: deliberate temporary simplifications taken for speed during early development, each with the condition/trigger that should prompt tightening. Mark the relaxation at its rule with the repo `early-stage` token; actionable tightening work goes to `docs/backlog/` as an `EB-N` item, referenced from here.

## Form
Prioritized list or table (item · impact · mitigation or tightening trigger · link).

## Do not put here
- Permanent conscious trade-offs that have no tightening trigger (those are decisions → §4/§9, or documented "conscious choices"). Product backlog features (→ `docs/backlog/`).

## Related chapters
§10 (quality at risk), §4/§9 (decisions that incur debt).
