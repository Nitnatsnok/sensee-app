# Chapter 8 — Crosscutting Concepts (Сквозные концепции)

## Purpose
Describe the principal regulations and solution ideas that apply across multiple parts of the system — they ensure conceptual integrity. Examples: domain models, architecture/design patterns, technology rules, implementation rules.

## Required / recommended subsections
- One subsection per concept. A non-mandatory grouping: domain concepts; user-experience; safety/security; architecture & design patterns; under-the-hood; development concepts; operational concepts.

## Form
Concept docs, model fragments with diagrams, code/usage examples, references — at the level needed for conceptual integrity.

## Do not put here
- One-off local decisions (→ §9 if significant, otherwise near the code). Full static structure (→ §5).

## Related chapters
Touches §3–§7 and §10; concepts often realize quality goals (§1/§10). In Sensee, the domain model lives in `docs/domain/` (information model, grammar taxonomy) and is linked, not restated.
