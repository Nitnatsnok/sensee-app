# Chapter 4 — Solution Strategy (Стратегия решения)

## Purpose
Summarize the fundamental decisions and solution strategies that shape the architecture: technology choices, top-level decomposition, approaches to achieving the key quality goals, and relevant organizational decisions.

## Required / recommended subsections
- Short, motivated statements of the key decisions, tied back to the problem statement, quality goals (§1), and constraints (§2). Defer detail to the later sections.

## Form
Brief prose or a compact table (decision · rationale). Keep it short — this is a summary, not the full rationale.

## Do not put here
- Full decision records with alternatives and consequences (→ §9 / `docs/adr/`). §9 must not duplicate §4 and vice versa: §4 is the short summary, §9 is the traceable record.

## Related chapters
§1 (Goals), §2 (Constraints), §5/§6/§7/§8 (where the strategy is realized), §9 (Decisions).
