# Chapter 2 — Architecture Constraints (Архитектурные ограничения)

## Purpose
Document requirements that constrain architects' freedom of design and implementation decisions, or the development process — constraints are sometimes valid across the whole organization.

## Required / recommended subsections
- Group constraints by kind: technical; organizational & political; conventions (coding, versioning, documentation, naming standards).

## Form
Simple tables of constraints, each with a short explanation and its consequence for the architecture.

## Do not put here
- Self-imposed solution decisions (→ §4). Quality goals (→ §1/§10).

## Related chapters
§1 (Goals), §4 (Solution Strategy works within these constraints).
